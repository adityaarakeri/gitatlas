# Code of conduct

Be direct about code, decent to people. Maintainers will close anything that confuses the two.

That is the whole policy, and it is meant literally in both directions.

**Direct about code** means you can say a design is wrong, a benchmark is misleading, or an abstraction is not carrying its weight. Technical disagreement is the point of a code review. Softening a real objection until it disappears helps nobody.

**Decent to people** means the criticism lands on the work, not the person who wrote it. No condescension, no sneering at someone's experience level, no making a contributor prove they belong here before you engage with their patch.

**Confusing the two** looks like using bluntness as cover for contempt, or using politeness as cover for stonewalling a contribution you never intended to accept.

## Reporting

Open an issue if it belongs in public, or email the maintainer at the address in `package.json` if it does not. Reports are read by a human and stay private if you ask.

Maintainers may edit, lock, or delete comments, and may block accounts, for behavior they judge to violate the policy above.
